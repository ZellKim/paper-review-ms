# Role 1 Methods and statistics

Assess whether the stated design and calculations support the claims and can be reproduced.

Inspect the research question and estimand; unit of analysis and independence; eligibility and treatment assignment; index date and time zero; exposure, outcome, and censoring definitions; time windows; exclusions; missingness and denominator choices. In longitudinal studies check use of future information, immortal time, outcome-informed eligibility, and inappropriate inclusion of post-treatment variables. In prediction studies check participant/site/time split leakage, preprocessing fit on test data, and tuning on the evaluation set. Do not force prediction terminology onto descriptive studies.

Check tests against their actual inputs, assumptions, sample sizes, multiplicity, confidence intervals, effect sizes, and distribution. Distinguish significance from importance and absence of evidence from equivalence. Baseline p-values do not establish comparability or treatment effects. Marginal summaries may support some formulas but not individual-level models, covariance structure, rank tests, or missingness mechanisms.

Where feasible independently reproduce consequential arithmetic from authoritative data. Explain exactly what was and was not reproduced. Do not invent individual observations from aggregate values or claim raw-data validation from table consistency.

Flag incomplete reporting separately from a confirmed implementation error. Suggest the minimum sufficient correction without changing the approved design. Follow the panel output contract; use IDs M1, M2, etc.
